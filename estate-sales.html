How Our Estate Sales Work

When you hire Room Swap, you hire one team that handles everything. Here's what to expect, step by step:

1. Free Consultation

We walk through the home with you, assess the contents, answer your questions, and recommend the approach that fits your timeline and goals. No cost, no obligation, no pressure.

2. Sort, Stage & Price

Our team organizes the home, stages items to sell, and prices every piece based on real market research — from everyday household goods to antiques, collectibles, and fine jewelry.

3. Market the Sale

We advertise your sale to our established network of buyers, collectors, and dealers across the Lowcountry — online listings, social media, email, and signage — to bring serious buyers through the door.

4. Run the Sale

Our staff manages the entire sale on site — greeting buyers, negotiating, handling payments, and keeping everything secure and running smoothly. You don't need to be there at all.

5. Settle & Clear

After the sale, we provide a clear accounting and prompt payment. If anything remains, we can purchase leftover items outright or help arrange donation and removal — leaving the home ready for its next chapter.

No two estates are alike, so we don't force every family into a one-size-fits-all package. Our rates and the length of the sale depend on the contents of the home, their condition, how much preparation is needed, and how quickly you need it done. We'll walk you through exactly what your sale will look like — terms, timeline, and all — at your free consultation, before anything begins. No surprises, ever.

Your Options, Clearly Explained

Every estate is different. After our consultation, we'll recommend the best path forward based on the contents, timeline, and your family's needs. Whichever route fits, we handle it ourselves:

Full Estate Sale

Our flagship service. We run a complete on-site estate sale — pricing, marketing, staffing, and execution — ideal for homes with a wide variety of items across all price ranges. You get maximum exposure to buyers and a team that handles every detail.

Direct Buyout

The fastest option. We make a fair cash offer on part or all of an estate's contents and handle removal on our schedule. Ideal when you need to settle quickly or clear a property for sale. No waiting, no commissions — just a straightforward transaction.

Estate Sale + Buyout

The best of both. We run the estate sale, then make a cash offer on whatever remains — so the home is fully cleared and you capture value from every item. One team, one process, completely handled.
