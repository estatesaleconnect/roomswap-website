Sell Your Vintage & Name-Brand Clothing in Holly Hill

Room Swap now buys clothing outright — by appointment only. Book a time below, bring your pieces in, and walk out paid for everything we buy.

This is not consignment. We don't take clothing on consignment. We purchase the pieces we choose on the spot, so there's no waiting for items to sell and no consignment split.

Please read before booking

Everything we buy is resold online, where buyers expect clean, ready-to-wear pieces that match what's selling right now. That means we're picky:

  • The right trend — styles people are searching for today
  • The right brand — vintage labels and sought-after names (see list below)
  • Clean condition with almost no flaws — freshly washed, no stains, holes, odors, or heavy wear

This isn't a donation drop-off or a thrift store bulk buy. If an item doesn't meet all three, we'll kindly pass and it goes home with you.

What We're Buying

Vintage T-Shirts

Band and concert tees, Harley-Davidson dealer tees, single-stitch tees, vintage sports and NASCAR, tourist and souvenir tees, beer and brand logos, cartoon and movie graphics — especially from the '80s, '90s, and early 2000s.

Harley-Davidson

Harley sweatshirts, hoodies, tees, leather and denim jackets, vests, and riding gear. Dealer-location shirts and older pieces with great graphics are at the top of our list.

Vintage Sweatshirts & Jackets

Crewnecks and hoodies with college, NFL, NBA, MLB, and team graphics, varsity and Starter jackets, windbreakers, and fleece. Made-in-USA tags are a plus.

Workwear & Outdoor

Carhartt, Dickies, Wrangler, Levi's denim, Patagonia, The North Face, Columbia, L.L.Bean, Filson, and other quality workwear and outdoor brands.

On-Trend Name Brands

Current, in-demand labels in like-new condition — think Nike, Adidas, Ralph Lauren, Tommy Hilfiger, Lululemon, and similar brands with real resale demand.

Western & Specialty

Pearl-snap shirts, western wear, quality leather, and unique one-of-a-kind vintage pieces with character.

Please note: We buy clothing outright only — we do not accept clothing on consignment.

What we can't buy: Fast fashion and mall basics (Shein, Old Navy, Walmart and store brands, etc.); anything with stains, holes, pilling, odors (smoke, pet, or musty), stretched-out collars, broken zippers, or heavy fading; undergarments and swimwear; items that aren't freshly laundered.

How Clothing Buys Work

1. Book a Time

Pick a day and time on the calendar below. We'll call or text to confirm your appointment.

2. Prep Your Pieces

Wash and fold everything and bring it in a bin, basket, or bag you can take home. Pull out anything with flaws before you come — it saves us both time.

3. Get Paid on the Spot

We go through your items with you and make an outright offer on the pieces we can sell online. Accept it and you're paid right then — no consignment, no waiting. Anything we pass on goes back home with you.
