# Room Swap Consignments: Brand Notes

Everything below comes from the live website source in this repo (`styles.css`, `index.html`, `sell.html`, `logo.png`, `/uploads`).

## Logo
- **Primary logo:** `logo.png` in the repo root. It has a green armchair icon, "Room Swap Consignments" in dark-brown serif, and a green "FURNITURE & HOME DECOR" bar.
- **Ad version:** `ads/src/img/logo.png`. It's the same logo, trimmed and with a transparent background. It sits on an off-white card so it reads over photos.
- **Website header wordmark:** "ROOM SWAP / CONSIGNMENTS / Where Modern Meets Vintage", set in Playfair Display with green side brackets.
- Every ad pairs the logo with **"HOLLY HILL, SC"** in spaced caps between two short green rules.

## Fonts
- **Playfair Display** (700/900) for headlines, CTAs, and the wordmark.
- **Crimson Text** (400/600/700) for body text, tags, and "Holly Hill, SC".
- Both are Google Fonts, loaded the same way the website loads them.

## Style
- The site calls its look "rustic industrial": a warm cream background, dark-brown serif type, forest green accents, and real showroom photos (concrete floors, framed art, mixed modern and vintage pieces).
- Voice: friendly, local, plain-spoken. Examples: "No yard sales, no hassle." "We handle everything." "You get paid when it sells."
- Tagline: *Where Modern Meets Vintage.*
