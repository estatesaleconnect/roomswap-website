Welcome to Room Swap — Holly Hill's Favorite Consignment Shop

Room Swap Consignments is Holly Hill's go-to destination for modern furniture, vintage antiques, and everything in between. Our 4,000 square foot showroom on Old State Road is part antique store, part vintage shop, part modern furniture gallery — hand-selected pieces that bring real character to any space, at prices that make sense. If you've been searching for a quality consignment or antique store in the Holly Hill area, you've found it.

Whether you're furnishing your first home, hunting for a one-of-a-kind vintage find, searching for a specific modern piece, or ready to turn your unwanted furniture into cash, we make it easy. We buy quality items outright and offer hassle-free consignment — and unlike a typical thrift store, every piece on our floor has been evaluated for quality before it gets here. Serving Holly Hill, Charleston, Summerville, Goose Creek, Moncks Corner, Orangeburg, St. George, Santee, and the greater Lowcountry area.

What You'll Find at Room Swap

Modern & Vintage Furniture

Common Questions

What is Room Swap Consignments?

Room Swap Consignments is a curated antique, vintage and modern consignment store at 8531 Old State Road in Holly Hill, South Carolina. The 4,000 sq ft showroom carries furniture, antiques, home decor, jewelry, books and vinyl records, and the inventory changes daily. Room Swap also buys items outright and runs full-service estate sales.

Is Room Swap a thrift store?

Not exactly. Prices are secondhand prices, but unlike a donation-based thrift store, every piece at Room Swap is hand-selected and inspected for quality and condition before it goes on the floor. Most items are consigned by local sellers or bought from estates. Consignment vs. thrift store: what’s the difference?.

Where is Room Swap located, and how far is it from Charleston and Orangeburg?

Room Swap is at 8531 Old State Road, Holly Hill, SC 29059, in eastern Orangeburg County near Lake Marion. It is about 30 minutes from Orangeburg and roughly an hour from Charleston, and an easy drive from Santee, Eutawville, St. George, Moncks Corner and Summerville. Get directions.

What are Room Swap's hours?

Tuesday through Friday 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday. No appointment is needed to shop or to bring items in during business hours.

How do I sell or consign furniture at Room Swap?

Text photos of your items to 843-900-1412 for a free evaluation. You can consign (you receive 40% of the sale price when the item sells, paid by check, Venmo or PayPal) or, for many items, sell outright with same-day payment. Room Swap also buys gold, silver, jewelry and entire estates. Two ways to get paid.
