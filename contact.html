Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment to visit Room Swap Consignments?

No appointment needed during regular business hours (Tuesday–Friday, 12–5 PM, and Saturday, 10 AM–5 PM). Just stop by! For large estate pickups or after-hours consultations, give us a call or text to schedule.

What should I bring for a consignment evaluation?

For consignment evaluation, bring photos of larger items or the actual pieces if they're portable. You can also text photos to 843-900-1412 for a quick assessment. We can assess most items on the spot.

How much do consignors get paid at Room Swap?

Our standard consignor payout is 40% of the sale price, paid when your item sells. Payouts are available by check, Venmo, or PayPal. See how consignment works.

What items does Room Swap Consignments accept?

We accept quality furniture in good condition (modern, vintage, and antique), home decor such as lamps, artwork, mirrors, and rugs, collectible books and vintage vinyl records, and vintage apparel, jewelry, collectibles, and art. We typically don't accept items with significant damage, stains, or wear; outdated electronics; incomplete furniture sets; mattresses; china hutches; or used appliances.

Does Room Swap buy furniture and jewelry outright?

Yes. In addition to consignment, we buy quality furniture, estate contents (one piece or an entire home), and gold, silver, and jewelry outright. Offers are based on current market value, and same-day payment is available. Learn how to sell to us.

Does Room Swap run estate sales?

Yes. We run full-service estate sales — sorting, pricing, staging, marketing, staffing, and sale-day management — starting with a free in-home consultation. We also offer direct estate buyouts. We serve Holly Hill, Charleston, Summerville, Goose Creek, Moncks Corner, Orangeburg, St. George, Santee, and the greater South Carolina Lowcountry. Learn about our estate sales.

What forms of payment does Room Swap accept?

We accept cash, credit cards, Venmo, and PayPal.

Does Room Swap Consignments deliver furniture?

Contact us about delivery options for larger furniture purchases. We can discuss arrangements based on your location and item size.

How quickly do consignment items sell?

It varies by item, but quality pieces priced right typically move within a few days to a few weeks. We'll keep you updated on interest and pricing adjustments if needed.
