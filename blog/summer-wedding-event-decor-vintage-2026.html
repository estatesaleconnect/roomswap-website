Summer Wedding & Event Decor on a Budget: Shop Vintage at Room Swap

Summer is wedding season in the South Carolina Lowcountry — and with it comes a wave of showers, rehearsal dinners, graduation parties, and backyard celebrations. If you're planning one, you already know how fast a decor budget disappears. The good news? Some of the most beautiful, photograph-worthy event styling out there comes straight from a consignment shop. This summer, before you order mass-produced decor online, stop by Room Swap Consignments in Holly Hill and shop vintage.

Vintage and antique decor brings something rental companies and big-box party stores simply can't: genuine character, warmth, and a one-of-a-kind look that makes a celebration feel personal. And because you're buying rather than renting, you can resell or keep the pieces afterward.

Why Vintage Decor Is a Wedding Planner's Secret Weapon

  • It's budget-friendly: Consignment prices are a fraction of rental fees and retail decor — and there are no per-item return deadlines.
  • It photographs beautifully: The patina of real vintage glass, brass, and wood gives photos a depth that plastic decor never will.
  • It's endlessly unique: Mismatched-on-purpose is the whole look right now. No two pieces have to match, which means no two tables have to either.
  • It's sustainable: Reusing beautiful old pieces is the greenest way to style an event — and you can pass them along afterward.

What to Hunt for at the Shop

Glassware, Vases, and Milk Glass

Vintage milk glass, colored glass bottles, mason jars, crystal, and assorted vases make stunning centerpieces and bud-vase clusters. Mix heights and shapes down a long farmhouse table for an effortless, collected look that florists charge a premium to recreate.

China, Silver, and Serving Pieces

Estate china, sterling and silver-plate trays, tiered stands, and punch bowls are perfect for showers, tea parties, and dessert tables. A stack of beautiful mismatched plates is a bridal-shower favorite — and far cheaper than renting a matched set.

Frames, Mirrors, and Signage

Ornate vintage frames make gorgeous table numbers, seating charts, and welcome signs. An antique mirror written on with a paint pen becomes an instant, elegant signboard. These are some of the easiest, highest-impact DIY projects in event styling.

Furniture for Lounge and Photo Areas

A vintage settee, a pair of wingback chairs, an old trunk, or a weathered ladder creates the kind of styled lounge and photo-backdrop moments couples love. Wooden crates, ladders, and shutters are endlessly useful as risers and displays.

Lanterns, Candlesticks, and Lighting

Brass and silver candlesticks, vintage lanterns, and hurricane lamps bring instant romance to an evening reception. Cluster them in odd numbers for that warm, golden-hour glow.

Baskets, Crates, and Galvanized Pieces

Perfect for programs, favors, flip-flop stations, and flower displays. Galvanized buckets and tubs also double as drink coolers for a casual Lowcountry summer celebration.

Styling Tips for a Vintage Summer Celebration

  • Pick a loose palette: Let two or three colors guide your hunt so the mismatched pieces still feel cohesive.
  • Shop early and often: Our inventory changes daily, so start collecting a few weeks out. The earlier you begin, the better your selection.
  • Buy in clusters: Groups of vases, candlesticks, and frames read as intentional design rather than odds and ends.
  • Think resale: After the event, you can consign the pieces right back with us — or keep your favorites at home as a memento.
