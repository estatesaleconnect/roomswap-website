Make Room for the Holidays: Sell or Consign Your Furniture in Holly Hill This Fall

Holiday guests are coming, the new tree needs a corner, and that extra dresser in the spare room has to go somewhere. If you're searching "sell furniture near me" or "who buys used furniture near Holly Hill," fall is the best time of year to do it — for you and for your wallet.

Here's why the next few weeks are prime selling season in the SC Lowcountry, what's in demand right now, and how to turn the things you don't use into holiday cash with Room Swap Consignments.

Why Fall Is the Best Time to Sell

  • Buyers are shopping for hosting. Dining tables, chairs, sideboards, and guest-room furniture are in demand before Thanksgiving and Christmas (see what hosts are looking for in our Thanksgiving hosting guide).
  • Gift shoppers want one-of-a-kind pieces. Decor, jewelry, vinyl records, and collectibles move fast between Small Business Saturday (November 28) and Christmas.
  • Holiday decor sells in season. Silver, brass, crystal, and holiday china sell best in October and November — not in July.
  • You get the space back right when you need it. Clear the guest room before the guests arrive, not after.
  • Holiday cash. Turn the furniture you're not using into money for gifts, travel, or the January bills.

What's Selling Right Now

Dining and Entertaining Furniture

Dining tables (especially with leaves), sets of chairs, sideboards, buffets, bar carts, and console tables. If it helps someone host a crowd, it's in demand.

Guest-Room Furniture

Bed frames, headboards, nightstands, dressers, and quality accent chairs. Families getting ready for overnight guests shop these hard in the fall. (Note: we don't accept mattresses.)

Silver, Brass, Crystal, and Decor

Sterling and silver plate, brass candlesticks, cut crystal, mirrors, lamps, and framed artwork. These are the pieces holiday decorators hunt for — our vintage Christmas decor guide shows why.

Gold, Silver, and Jewelry

We buy gold, silver, and jewelry outright. Bring pieces by for a free evaluation and an offer based on current market rates.

Vinyl Records

Record collections are one of the most popular gifts of the season. If you have a box of LPs in the closet, we buy vinyl collections — even if you don't know what's in them.

Antiques, Vintage, and Collectibles

True antiques, mid-century pieces, vintage apparel, and unique finds with character all do well with gift and decor shoppers.

How to Get Your Furniture Ready

  • Clean it. A wipe-down and polish can make a real difference in what a piece sells for.
  • Tighten and repair small things. Loose knobs, wobbly legs, and sticky drawers are quick fixes.
  • Keep sets together. Chairs with their table, nightstands with their dresser — complete sets sell better.
  • Take good photos. Natural light, the whole piece, plus close-ups of any maker's marks or damage.

Our full guide to preparing furniture for consignment has more tips.
