Summer Declutter: Best Items to Consign at Room Swap This Season

Summer in the South Carolina Lowcountry and Lakes region brings more than heat and humidity — it brings opportunity. People move. Families settle estates. Lake house owners refresh their spaces. Downsizers finally tackle the rooms they've been putting off. And all of that activity creates the best consignment season of the year. If your garage, spare bedroom, or storage unit is full of quality furniture and decor you no longer need, now is the time to bring it to Room Swap Consignments in Holly Hill and turn it into cash.

We serve sellers from across the region — Orangeburg, Santee, Manning, Summerville, Elloree, Harleyville, St. George, and everywhere in between. Whether you're clearing a lake house on Lake Marion or Lake Moultrie, settling a family estate in Dorchester County, or just doing a long-overdue summer refresh in the Lowcountry, we want to hear from you.

Why Summer Is the Best Time to Consign

The consignment market heats up right alongside the temperature every summer — and for good reason. Here's what drives the surge:

  • Summer moves: More people relocate between June and August than any other time of year. That means buyers are actively furnishing new homes — and sellers need to move their old furniture fast.
  • Estate activity: Many families gather over summer holidays and finally address inherited homes, prompting a wave of quality estate furniture hitting the market.
  • Lake house and vacation property transitions: Property sales along Lake Marion and Lake Moultrie spike in summer, and new owners often want fresh furnishings rather than inheriting the old ones.
  • The "fresh start" feeling: Summer inspires people to declutter and update their spaces. That couch you've been staring at all winter? It's time for it to go — and to put cash in your pocket instead of hauling it to the dump.

What Sells Fast at Room Swap This Summer

We're selective because our buyers expect quality, but that works in your favor too — quality pieces sell faster and at better prices in a curated consignment environment than anywhere else. Here's what we're moving quickly right now:

Solid Wood Furniture

Bedroom suites, dining room sets, sideboards, buffets, china cabinets, and bookcases in solid wood are always in demand. Brands like Ethan Allen, Drexel Heritage, Thomasville, Henredon, and Broyhill move especially well. If it has dovetail joints and real wood construction, bring it in. Summer buyers are actively furnishing homes and they know quality when they see it.

Outdoor and Porch Furniture

This is prime season for porch rockers, outdoor dining sets, iron furniture, wicker pieces, and screened-porch seating. Lowcountry living revolves around the porch and the dock — if you have quality outdoor furniture you're no longer using, summer is the ideal time to consign it while demand is at its peak.

Vintage and Antique Pieces

Mid-century modern, farmhouse antiques, chippendale, and Victorian pieces are extremely popular with both local buyers and the growing number of visitors who stop in while traveling through the region. Unique vintage items that tell a story are especially sought after right now. Don't assume something is "too old" — often the older and more distinctive, the better.

Home Decor and Accent Pieces

Lamps, mirrors, artwork, decorative bowls, vases, and accent furniture like side tables and consoles fly off our floor in summer as buyers furnish or refresh their spaces. Quality decor from name brands or with clear craftsmanship sells particularly well.

China, Silver, and Glassware

Estate china sets, sterling silver flatware, crystal glassware, and decorative silver pieces are in consistent demand year-round — but summer estate activity drives extra supply and buyer interest. If you've inherited a full china set or a sterling silver service and have no use for it, don't let it sit in a cabinet. Bring it in.

Artwork and Wall Decor

Original paintings, framed prints, sculptures, and quality decorative wall pieces move well all year, with a summer spike as new homeowners and lake property owners furnish their walls. We especially welcome Lowcountry-themed art, landscapes, and coastal pieces.

How Consigning at Room Swap Works

We make the process as simple as possible. Here's the basic flow when you sell to us or consign with us:

  • Contact us first: Call or text us at 843-900-1412, or send us a message with photos of what you have. This saves you a trip if items aren't a good fit.
  • Bring items in: We're open Tuesday through Saturday, 12–5 PM at 8531 Old State Road, Holly Hill, SC 29059. For larger loads or estate items, we can arrange a pickup or in-home visit.
  • We price and sell: Our team prices your items fairly based on current market demand and manages the sale entirely — including marketing to our buyer network across the region.
  • You get paid: When your items sell, you receive your consignment percentage. No hassle, no Facebook Marketplace negotiations, no strangers in your home.

For large loads — full rooms of furniture, estate contents, or multiple truckloads — we also offer outright buyouts where we purchase your items directly. This is the fastest way to clear a space with no waiting on the consignment timeline.
