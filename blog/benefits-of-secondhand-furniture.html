5 Benefits of Buying Secondhand Furniture

In a world of fast furniture and flat-pack assembly, secondhand and consignment furniture offers something increasingly rare: quality, character, and value. Whether you're a first-time homeowner in Summerville, redecorating your Charleston bungalow, or furnishing a lake house near Santee, here are five reasons buying pre-owned furniture is one of the smartest moves you can make.

1. Save Serious Money

This is the benefit that gets most people in the door — and keeps them coming back. Secondhand furniture typically costs 50–70% less than the same piece bought new. A solid wood dining table that retails for $2,000 might sell for $600–$800 at a consignment shop. A quality leather sofa that costs $3,000 new? You could find one in excellent condition for under $1,000.

And unlike big-box furniture stores where "sales" often just bring prices down to what they should have been in the first place, the savings at consignment shops are real. You're getting genuine quality at a fraction of the original price.

The math speaks for itself: a family furnishing a living room, dining room, and bedroom could easily save $5,000–$10,000 by shopping secondhand compared to buying everything new. That's money you can put toward renovations, experiences, or simply keeping in the bank.

2. Get Better Quality for Less

Here's something most people don't realize: a used piece of quality furniture is almost always better than a new piece of cheap furniture at the same price point. A $400 pre-owned Ethan Allen dresser will outlast a $400 new dresser from a budget retailer by decades.

Older furniture was often built during an era when craftsmanship mattered more than profit margins. Solid hardwood construction, dovetail joints, real wood veneer, and quality hardware were standard — not premium upgrades. When you buy secondhand, you're essentially getting yesterday's quality at today's budget prices.

At Room Swap Consignments, every piece on our showroom floor has been inspected for quality. We don't accept furniture that's falling apart or past its useful life. What you'll find are pieces that have years — often decades — of life left in them.

3. Find One-of-a-Kind Pieces

Walk into any chain furniture store and you'll see the same styles in every location across the country. Walk into a consignment shop, and every visit is different. The inventory is constantly rotating, which means there's always something new to discover.

Secondhand shopping is where you find the pieces that make a home feel like your home — a mid-century credenza with perfect lines, a farmhouse table with real patina, an antique mirror that becomes the focal point of your entryway. These are the pieces that spark conversations and give a room personality.

If you value a home that reflects your personal style rather than a catalog page, consignment and secondhand furniture is where you'll find it.

4. Reduce Your Environmental Impact

The furniture industry has a significant environmental footprint. Manufacturing new furniture requires raw materials, energy, water, and chemicals — and generates waste at every stage. According to the EPA, Americans throw away over 12 million tons of furniture every year, most of which ends up in landfills.

Every piece of secondhand furniture you buy is one less piece manufactured and one less piece in a landfill. It's one of the simplest and most impactful forms of recycling — you're giving a quality item a second life while reducing demand for new production.

Buying secondhand also means zero packaging waste. No styrofoam, no cardboard boxes, no plastic wrap. You pick up a piece of furniture that's already built, already broken in, and ready to use in your home today.

5. Support Your Local Community

When you shop at a local consignment store, your money stays in the community. It supports the shop, it supports the local consignors who earn income from their pieces, and it supports the local economy in a way that ordering from an online mega-retailer never will.

Consignment shops are community hubs. They're places where neighbors meet, where stories get passed along with furniture, and where the character of a community is reflected in the items on the floor. At Room Swap, we know many of our customers by name, and we love hearing about how a piece found the perfect spot in someone's home.

Shopping local also means you can see, touch, and sit on furniture before you buy it. No gambling on online photos, no waiting weeks for delivery, and no assembly required. You find what you love, load it up, and it's in your home the same day.
