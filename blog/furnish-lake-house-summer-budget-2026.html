How to Furnish a Lake House for Summer on a Budget

Summer on Lake Marion or Lake Moultrie is what Lowcountry dreams are made of — boat days, dock fishing, and porches full of family. But anyone who's furnished a lake house knows the secret: it takes a lot of furniture, and the last thing you want to do is put fragile, expensive retail pieces in a space full of wet swimsuits, sandy feet, and rotating houseguests. The smart move? Furnish it with quality consignment and vintage finds from Room Swap Consignments in Holly Hill and keep your budget for the boat.

A lake house should feel relaxed, lived-in, and worry-free. That's exactly the kind of casual, characterful look that secondhand furniture delivers best — and at a fraction of the cost of furnishing a second home from scratch at retail prices.

Why Consignment Is Perfect for a Lake House

A lake house has a completely different set of needs than your primary home, and consignment fits every one of them:

  • You don't want to baby it: Pieces that already have a little wear won't stress you out when a kid drags a tackle box across the floor.
  • You need a lot, fast: Furnishing an entire second home at retail adds up quickly. Consignment lets you fill rooms for a fraction of the price.
  • Character beats matchy-matchy: The best lake houses look collected over time, not ordered from a single catalog. A mix of vintage and modern is the whole point.
  • Solid construction holds up: Real wood and vintage build quality survive the humidity and heavy use a lake house dishes out.

Room-by-Room: What to Look For

The Living and Gathering Space

This is where everyone lands after a day on the water. Look for a sturdy, comfortable sofa and a mix of armchairs that can take a beating. A solid wood coffee table — ideally one that already shows some honest wear — is ideal for propped-up feet and dripping glasses. Add a vintage bookshelf or cabinet for games, puzzles, and life jackets.

Bedrooms for the Crowd

Lake houses sleep a lot of people. Solid wood bed frames, dressers, and nightstands from brands like Broyhill, Thomasville, and Ethan Allen show up regularly on our floor and cost a fraction of new. Mixing different bedroom sets across guest rooms actually adds to the charm — nobody expects a lake house to match.

The Dining Area

A big, durable dining table is non-negotiable for fish fries and pancake breakfasts. Farmhouse tables and solid wood dining sets are perfect — and a few mismatched chairs only add to the easygoing vibe. Keep an eye out for extra folding pieces and a sideboard for serving.

The Porch and Dock

Don't forget the most important room of all. Porch rockers, wicker seating, iron bistro sets, and weather-tolerant tables turn the porch and dock into the real heart of the lake house. (We dive deeper into outdoor pieces in our guide to summer porch and patio decor.)

Lake House Decor That Sets the Mood

The finishing touches are what make a lake house feel like a getaway. Hunt for:

  • Coastal and lake-themed art: Landscapes, water scenes, and vintage prints set the tone instantly.
  • Lamps and lighting: Warm, layered lighting beats harsh overheads for those long summer evenings.
  • Baskets and storage: Vintage baskets and trunks corral towels, toys, and gear while looking great doing it.
  • Quilts and textiles: A stack of quilts handles cool nights and adds that cozy, collected feel.
  • Mirrors: They bounce light and make smaller lake cottages feel open and airy.

A Smart Strategy for Furnishing Fast

If you're starting from an empty lake house, a few tips will save you time and money:

  • Prioritize the big anchors first: Beds, the main sofa, and the dining table. Build out from there.
  • Visit often: Our 4,000 sq ft showroom changes daily, so the more you stop in, the more you'll find. Quality lake-ready pieces move quickly in summer.
  • Call ahead: Tell us what you're after at 843-900-1412 and we'll keep an eye out as new inventory comes in.
  • Ask about buyouts and larger lots: If you need to furnish several rooms at once, we may be able to help you do it in fewer trips.
