Selling & Consigning FAQ

How do I get an offer or a consignment quote?

Text clear photos of your items to 843-900-1412 with a short description (brand, size, condition). We’ll reply with honest feedback on whether we can consign or buy them. You can also stop by during business hours with smaller pieces. Evaluations are free.

What's the difference between selling outright and consigning?

Selling outright means we pay you now and take on the risk of reselling the item, with same-day payment available. Consigning means we display and sell it for you and you receive 40% of the sale price when it sells: usually more money in total, but paid later. Our guide to the best way to sell used furniture compares every option.

Do you buy gold, silver and jewelry?

Yes. We buy gold, silver and jewelry outright at fair prices based on current market rates. Bring items by during business hours for a free evaluation, and see our guide to selling gold and jewelry near Holly Hill.

Can you pick up large furniture or a houseful of items?

For larger furniture, collections or estate contents, contact us to discuss pickup options. For whole homes, we also offer estate buyouts and full-service estate sales.

What won't Room Swap accept?

Items with significant damage, stains or wear; outdated electronics; incomplete furniture sets; mattresses; china hutches; and used appliances.
