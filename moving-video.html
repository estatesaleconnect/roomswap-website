Tips for Great Moving Videos

Use Good Lighting

Natural light near a window gives furniture photos warmth and depth that animation really brings to life.

Fill the Frame

Get close to your subject. The Ken Burns and Zoom effects look most dramatic when the item fills most of the photo.

Try the Sweep for Rooms

The Cinematic Sweep is perfect for wide room shots — it pans across the full space like a professional walk-through.

Share on Facebook

Moving videos outperform still photos on Facebook. Upload your video directly to a post for the best reach.
